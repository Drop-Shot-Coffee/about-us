DROP SHOT COFFEE, INC.
About
A premium beverage company

Where the game meets the cup.

Drop Shot Coffee is building a premium beverage brand designed for the fastest-growing category in social athletics — pickleball and the wider world of court sports. We exist at the intersection of community, hospitality, and high-performance leisure.

Mission

To become the signature beverage of social court sports — a brand players recognize on the sideline, gather around between games, and associate with the culture they're helping create.

We craft coffee, tea, and functional drinks for athletes who play for the love of the game and the people they play with. The product is premium; the experience is communal; the standard is consistent across every venue we serve.

Ambition

Pickleball is our entry point. Court sports — padel, tennis, racquetball, and the social formats emerging around them — are the larger field. We are building Drop Shot Coffee to be the category-defining beverage brand for that field, starting in California and scaling to wherever the game is played.

Our model unites five pillars that, taken together, build a category no single coffee brand or venue operator has built before.

Standard

We hold ourselves to the standard of the venues we serve. Specialty-grade sourcing. Branded presentation. Trained service. Drop Shot is not a coffee cart with a logo — it is a hospitality experience built specifically for the rhythm of competitive play and the audience that surrounds it.

Built for the game. Built for the community around it.
